How it works

  1. Fill the SAD model (or simple template) in Excel, WPS or Google Sheets — or use the Form tab.
  2. Open it on the File tab. Check the insights and type any missing duty rates.
  3. Generate and download or share the SAD. It is the official model, filled in — ready to import into ASYCUDA.

Good to know

  • Private: files are read and created on this phone only. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Offline: after the first visit the app works without internet. Add it to your home screen from the browser menu.
  • Duty rates: come from your file or what you type — the phone can't look them up automatically. Use 🔎 to open the customs tariff.
  • Limits (ASYCUDA): description 49 characters, brand/model/size 24, packing codes 5 — longer text is shortened with a warning.
